## WHY THIS CHANGE

**Reason 1: Recognize Historic Collaboration**

This framework represents unprecedented multi-AI collaboration across competing organizations. Attribution ensures this historic achievement is recognized and documented.

**Reason 2: Maintain Integrity**

Attribution requirement helps prevent misrepresentation of the framework's origins and ensures modifications are clearly identified as such.

**Reason 3: Track Adoption**

Attribution allows tracking where and how the framework is being used, enabling learning from real-world implementation.

**Reason 4: Credit AI Contributors**

The six AI systems engaged seriously in constitutional development. CC BY 4.0 ensures their contributions are acknowledged, setting precedent for AI participation in governance frameworks.
