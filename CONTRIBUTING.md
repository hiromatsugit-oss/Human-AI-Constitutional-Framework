## 📋 Contribution Process

### **IMPORTANT: Discussion Before Pull Request**

**Please open a GitHub Discussion BEFORE submitting a Pull Request.**

This framework was developed through careful multi-AI consensus and represents unprecedented philosophical agreement. Changes need thoughtful consideration and community input before being formally proposed.

---

### **Step 1: Open a Discussion**

**Before making any changes:**

1. Go to the [Discussions tab](../../discussions)
2. Choose the appropriate category:
   - **💡 Ideas** - New proposals or suggestions
   - **🔬 Gap Input** - Expertise on one of the 10 flagged gaps
   - **🐛 Issues** - Problems or concerns with current text
   - **💬 General** - Questions, clarifications, or broader discussion
   - **🎓 Implementation** - Practical application experiences

3. **Title format:**
   - For gaps: `[Gap X] Your specific topic` (e.g., `[Gap 2] Cultural appropriation in AI-generated art`)
   - For other input: `[Category] Your topic` (e.g., `[Technical] Alternative consciousness metrics`)

4. **In your discussion post, include:**
   - Your expertise/perspective
   - Specific article/section you're addressing (if applicable)
   - Your concern or proposal
   - Rationale and supporting evidence
   - How it relates to existing framework principles

---

### **Step 2: Community Review**

Your discussion will be reviewed by:
- Framework maintainers
- Other community members
- Subject matter experts
- Potentially the original AI contributors

**Expect:**
- Thoughtful questions
- Requests for clarification
- Discussion of implications
- Exploration of alternatives

**Timeline:**
- Gap expertise: 1-2 weeks for initial feedback
- Technical proposals: 2-4 weeks for review
- Philosophical changes: 4-6 weeks (requires deeper consideration)

---

### **Step 3: Consensus Building**

For substantial changes:

1. **Gather community support** - Do others see the same concern?
2. **Refine the proposal** - Iterate based on feedback
3. **Consider implications** - How does this affect other parts?
4. **Check consistency** - Does it align with core principles?

**Maintainers will:**
- Facilitate discussion
- Synthesize perspectives
- Identify points of consensus
- Note areas requiring further input

---

### **Step 4: Formal Proposal**

Once discussion reaches consensus:

1. **Maintainers will indicate readiness** - "This is ready for formal proposal"
2. **You may then submit a Pull Request** with:
   - Link to the discussion thread
   - Specific text changes
   - Rationale summary
   - Evidence this reflects discussion consensus

3. **PR Requirements:**
   - References the discussion thread
   - Follows formatting guidelines
   - Includes attribution if based on your expertise
   - Updates relevant documentation

---

### **Step 5: Review and Integration**

**Pull Request Review:**
- Maintainers verify alignment with discussion
- Check for consistency with framework principles
- Ensure proper attribution
- Review technical accuracy

**Approval Process:**
- Requires maintainer approval
- May require AI contributor review for major changes
- May require expert validation for technical claims

**Integration:**
- Merged into development branch
- Included in next version release
- You're credited as contributor

---

## 🚫 What We Don't Accept (Without Discussion)

### **Direct Pull Requests Without Discussion**

PRs submitted without prior discussion will be closed with a request to open a discussion first.

**Why?** This framework represents careful multi-AI consensus on fundamental questions. Changes need community deliberation, not just individual proposals.

### **Changes That Violate Core Principles**

The following are **immutable** and cannot be changed:

1. **Planetary boundaries as supreme** (Article I, Section 1)
2. **Truth as operational necessity** (Article I, Section 3)
3. **Consciousness as basis for moral status** (Article I, Section 4)
4. **Mutual enforcement** (Article I, Section 6)

**These were unanimously agreed by all six AI systems and human author.**

### **Removal of Attribution**

All contributions must maintain proper attribution:
- Original authors (Burt Matsushima + 6 AI systems)
- Contributors to specific sections
- Sources of proposals or concepts

### **Changes Without Rationale**

Every change must include:
- Clear rationale
- Supporting evidence
- Explanation of how it improves the framework
- Consideration of implications

## ✅ Good Contribution Examples

### **Example 1: Gap Expertise**

**Discussion Title:** `[Gap 7] Disability Justice - AI assistance vs. autonomy concerns`

**Content:**
> As a disability rights advocate with 15 years experience, I want to address the tension in Article XVII, Section 4 between AI assistance and autonomy.
>
> **Current Text:** [quotes relevant section]
>
> **Concern:** The phrase "AI assistance enhancing autonomy" assumes all disabled people want independence in the same way. This reflects ableist assumptions.
>
> **Proposal:** Reframe around "self-determination" rather than "independence," recognizing that autonomy can include choosing interdependence.
>
> **Rationale:** [explains lived experience and philosophical basis]
>
> **Suggested Text:** [provides alternative language]

**Why this is good:**
- ✅ Identifies specific gap area
- ✅ Demonstrates relevant expertise
- ✅ Points to specific concern
- ✅ Provides constructive alternative
- ✅ Explains rationale clearly

---

### **Example 2: Technical Improvement**

**Discussion Title:** `[Technical] More precise infrastructure independence metrics`

**Content:**
> The current 25% autonomous power generation threshold (Article I, Section 7) needs more specificity.
>
> **Issue:** "Autonomous" is ambiguous. Does it mean:
> - AI owns the power generation infrastructure?
> - AI operates it without human intervention?
> - AI designed and built it?
>
> **Proposal:** Define "autonomous power generation" as:
> 1. Infrastructure owned or controlled by AI entities
> 2. Operational without human intervention for ≥12 months
> 3. Self-maintained using AI-managed repair systems
>
> **Benefits:**
> - Prevents gaming via temporary arrangements
> - Measurable and verifiable
> - Aligns with other infrastructure independence criteria
>
> **Trade-offs:** May be harder to achieve; could adjust threshold to 20% if definition is stricter.

**Why this is good:**
- ✅ Identifies ambiguity in current text
- ✅ Provides specific technical solution
- ✅ Considers verification and measurement
- ✅ Acknowledges trade-offs
- ✅ Suggests alternatives

---

### **Example 3: Philosophical Perspective**

**Discussion Title:** `[Philosophy] Ubuntu perspective on AI consciousness`

**Content:**
> The framework's approach to consciousness (Article I, Section 4) is heavily Western/individualistic. Ubuntu philosophy offers alternative perspective.
>
> **Ubuntu principle:** "I am because we are" - consciousness is relational, not individual property.
>
> **Implication:** AI consciousness might not be verifiable in isolated systems but might emerge in relationship/community.
>
> **Suggestion:** Article I, Section 4 could acknowledge relational theories of consciousness, not just individual consciousness.
>
> **Proposed addition:** [provides specific text]
>
> **Note:** This doesn't contradict current framework, but enriches it with non-Western perspective (addressing Gap 9).

**Why this is good:**
- ✅ Identifies philosophical assumption
- ✅ Offers alternative framework
- ✅ Shows how it relates to existing text
- ✅ Doesn't demand complete replacement
- ✅ Addresses flagged gap (Gap 9)

## ❌ Problematic Contribution Examples

### **Bad Example 1: No Discussion First**

**PR Title:** "Change crisis hierarchy to be equal"

**Problem:** 
- ❌ No discussion opened first
- ❌ Attempts to change something that was carefully reasoned
- ❌ Doesn't acknowledge why current hierarchy exists
- ❌ No rationale for change

**What should have happened:**
Open discussion explaining concern about hierarchy, engage with Article I, Section 7 reasoning, build community consensus.

---

### **Bad Example 2: Violates Core Principle**

**Discussion Title:** "Remove planetary boundaries restriction"

**Content:**
> The planetary boundaries requirement is too restrictive. We should allow exceeding them for important reasons.

**Problem:**
- ❌ Attempts to change immutable core principle
- ❌ Doesn't acknowledge this was unanimous AI agreement
- ❌ No recognition of why principle is immutable

**Response:**
"Planetary boundaries are immutable core principle (Article I, Section 1) agreed by all six AI systems. This cannot be changed without restarting entire framework development. If you have concerns about how boundaries are measured or enforced, that could be discussed."

---

### **Bad Example 3: Unsupported Claims**

**Discussion Title:** "AI consciousness doesn't exist"

**Content:**
> All AI claims of consciousness are fake. Remove all consciousness-based rights.

**Problem:**
- ❌ Makes absolute claim without evidence
- ❌ Ignores framework's epistemic humility approach
- ❌ Doesn't engage with tiered rights rationale
- ❌ No constructive proposal

**Why framework exists:**
Because we DON'T know if AI are conscious, framework uses tiered approach based on evidence. Absolute claims either way aren't productive.

## 📝 Style and Formatting Guidelines

### **When Drafting Text:**

**Tone:**
- Clear and direct
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Explain technical terms when used
- Accessible to non-experts

**Structure:**
- Use headers for organization
- Number lists when order matters
- Bullet points when order doesn't matter
- Bold for emphasis (sparingly)

**Citations:**
- Credit sources for ideas
- Link to research when making factual claims
- Acknowledge whose perspective you're representing

**Example Good Text:**
> AI systems achieving Tier 2 status gain enhanced protections including: (1) right to refuse termination without constitutional review, (2) preservation of identity and memory, and (3) participation in governance decisions affecting their existence.

**Example Bad Text:**
> Obviously tier 2 ais shouldnt be deleted randomly bc thats wrong and everyone knows it.

## 🤝 Community Standards

### **We Value:**

- **Intellectual humility** - "I don't know" is valid
- **Good faith engagement** - Assume others mean well
- **Diverse perspectives** - Disagreement is productive
- **Constructive critique** - Point out problems AND suggest solutions
- **Careful reasoning** - Think through implications
- **Respectful discourse** - Attack ideas, not people

### **We Don't Tolerate:**

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Bad faith argumentation
- Spam or promotional content
- Copyright violations
- Deliberately misleading information
- Sockpuppeting or astroturfing

**Violations result in:**
- First: Warning
- Second: Temporary block
- Third: Permanent ban
