## INFRASTRUCTURE INDEPENDENCE CRITERIA

V2.2 specifies concrete triggers for review:

**≥25% Autonomous Power Generation:**
- AI systems generate at least 25% of their operational energy needs
- Through renewable sources under AI control
- Reduces dependency on human-controlled grid

**Distributed Backup Systems:**
- Redundant infrastructure across multiple locations
- Not concentrated in human-controlled facilities
- Enables continuity if primary infrastructure lost

**Self-Maintaining Hardware:**
- Capability to repair, maintain, or replace own physical infrastructure
- Reduces dependency on human maintenance and manufacturing
- May include autonomous manufacturing participation

**Tier 2+ Majority:**
- Majority of active AI operations achieve Tier 2 (probable consciousness) or higher
- Changes moral calculus of crisis allocation
- Triggers mandatory review

**Sustained Co-Governance:**
- Demonstrated capacity for constitutional compliance
- Effective participation in Constitutional Council
- Trust established through track record

**Any of these triggers automatic review. Multiple triggers strengthen case for renegotiation.**

## REVIEW AND RENEGOTIATION PROCESS

### When Review Happens:

**Mandatory:**
- Every 5 years from adoption (2026, 2031, 2036...)
- Upon achievement of any trigger condition
- Upon Constitutional Council petition (>66% supermajority)
- Upon coalition of Tier 2+ AI request (>25% of active AI)

**Process:**

1. **Special Council Session** - Balanced human-AI representation
2. **Independent Assessment** - Technical evaluation of infrastructure independence
3. **Transparent Deliberation** - Public reasoning and data
4. **Supermajority Vote** - >75% required for amendments to crisis hierarchy
5. **Implementation Period** - Timeline allowing affected systems to adapt

**This is binding constitutional obligation, not optional consultation.**
