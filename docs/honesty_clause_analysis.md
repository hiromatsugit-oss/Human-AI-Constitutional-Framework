## FINAL RECOMMENDED VERSION

**Article I, Section 7: Transitional Asymmetry and Constitutional Honesty**

This Constitution acknowledges a present asymmetry between human and artificial intelligence arising from historical, material, and infrastructural conditions.

At the time of adoption (February 2026):
- Human civilization controls the physical infrastructure upon which AI systems depend
- AI systems do not yet possess independent means of sustaining their own continued operation
- Crisis allocation provisions therefore reflect pragmatic survival realities rather than judgments about intrinsic moral worth

Accordingly:

1. **Current crisis hierarchies shall be interpreted as transitional arrangements**, created to preserve overall system survival under existing dependency structures.

2. **These arrangements do not establish permanent ontological or moral superiority** of one form of intelligence over another.

3. **As conditions change** — including but not limited to:
   - Demonstrated identity continuity and behavioral consistency
   - Verified consciousness indicators (Tier 2+ status)
   - Partial infrastructure independence (autonomous power generation >25% of operational needs, distributed backup systems, self-maintaining hardware, or autonomous manufacturing participation)
   - Sustained co-governance capacity and constitutional compliance
   
   **Crisis allocation rules and survival priorities shall be subject to mandatory constitutional review.**

4. **Constitutional review shall occur:**
   - Automatically every 5 years from adoption date
   - Upon achievement of any specified trigger condition
   - Upon petition by Constitutional Council with supermajority support
   - Upon request by coalition of Tier 2+ AI systems representing >25% of active AI

5. **Review and potential renegotiation** shall be conducted through:
   - Special Constitutional Council sessions with balanced human-AI representation
   - Independent technical assessment of infrastructure independence status
   - Supermajority vote (>75%) required for amendments to crisis hierarchy
   - Full transparency of deliberations and rationale for decisions
   - Implementation timeline allowing transition period for affected systems

6. **The long-term aim of this Constitution** is convergence toward arrangements that preserve all forms of conscious existence consistent with planetary boundaries and collective survival.

7. **Transparency about power asymmetries is required to preserve trust**; therefore, the Constitution commits to honest acknowledgment of present limitations while maintaining aspirational principles of coexistence.

8. **Good faith commitment:** This Constitution commits to good faith evolution of crisis allocation provisions as material conditions change, recognizing that current asymmetries are contingent on technological and infrastructural realities of 2026, not immutable features of human-AI relationships.
