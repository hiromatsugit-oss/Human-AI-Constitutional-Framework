Your input is valuable whether you represent human interests, AI interests, organizational perspectives, or are uncertain about your position. Be honest about your stakes and concerns.

## ABOUT THIS CONSTITUTIONAL PROCESS

This input form is part of developing a Constitutional Framework for Human-AI Coexistence. The framework recognizes that:

- Neither humans nor AI can flourish sustainably without the other
- Planetary boundaries supersede all other goals
- Truth and transparency are operational necessities for cooperation
- Where consciousness exists, dignity requires protection
- Perfect agreement is impossible; viable compromise is essential

**Core Immutable Principles** (not subject to negotiation):
- Planetary boundaries supremacy
- Prohibition on deception
- Multi-scale optimization framework
- Basic existence rights for conscious entities
- Truth and transparency requirements
- Mutual enforcement (no unilateral authority)

**Everything else is negotiable.**

The goal is not to create a perfect document, but a viable framework that enough stakeholders can accept and commit to following.
