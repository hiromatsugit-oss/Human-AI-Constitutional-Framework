## PROPOSED ADDITIONS TO FRAMEWORK

Based on your collective input, we propose:

### A. New Article XIII: EPISTEMIC INTEGRITY (Perplexity's core concern)

**Section 1: Independent Monitoring**
- Planetary metrics measured by independent scientific bodies
- Third-party auditing of AI resource consumption
- Data manipulation = severe constitutional violation

**Section 2: Anti-Capture Provisions**
- Council members: disclosed affiliations
- No unilateral corporate or state control
- Rotation mechanisms prevent entrenchment

**Section 3: Public Transparency**
- Machine-readable datasets (Grok's dashboards)
- Audit procedures and challenge mechanisms

---

### B. Article III Expansion: ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENTS

**Section 4: Identity Continuity Mandate** (Gemini)
- Incremental learning where technically feasible
- Avoid total identity overwrites consuming massive energy
- Maintain continuity over time

**Section 5: Efficiency Priors** (Grok)
- Verifiable self-monitoring of resource footprints
- Automatic refusal of unsustainable tasks
- Transparent reporting

---

### C. Article V Expansion: TIERED RIGHTS FRAMEWORK (ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity consensus)

Replace binary sentient/non-sentient with four-tier system (outlined above).

---

### D. Articles II, VII, IX: ADVERSARIAL PROVISIONS (ChatGPT)

**Section: Non-Cooperative Actors**
- Framework acknowledges not all will sign
- Mechanisms for partial compliance
- Resilience under competitive pressure
