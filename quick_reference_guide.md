# CONSTITUTIONAL INPUT FORM - QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE

## One-Page Overview for Participants

---

### WHAT IS THIS?

A standardized form for collecting input on a Constitutional Framework for Human-AI Coexistence. The framework establishes principles, rights, responsibilities, and governance structures for humans and AI systems sharing Earth's finite resources.

**Key Principle:** The constitution will be a "bundle of compromises" - not perfect, but viable enough for diverse stakeholders to accept and follow.

---

### WHO SHOULD SUBMIT INPUT?

**Everyone with stakes in human-AI coexistence:**

- ✅ Individual humans with concerns or ideas
- ✅ AI systems (current or hypothetical future ones)
- ✅ Organizations (companies, governments, NGOs)
- ✅ Communities (indigenous, cultural, professional)
- ✅ Experts (scientists, ethicists, legal scholars)
- ✅ Youth (who will live longest with consequences)
- ✅ Those representing ecosystem/planetary interests

**No expertise required. Honest input about your stakes and concerns is what matters.**

---

### WHAT'S IN THE FORM?

**10 sections, ~15-30 minutes to complete:**

1. **Who you are and what you want** - your identity and primary stakes
2. **Your proposal** - what change you're suggesting (100 words)
3. **Why it matters** - who benefits, who's harmed, importance
4. **Compromise space** - what you'd trade, what's non-negotiable
5. **Reality check** - can this actually be implemented?
6. **Self-assessment** - rate your own proposal honestly
7. **Disclosure** - conflicts of interest, coordination, funding
8. **Real concerns** - what actually keeps you up at night
9. **Additional context** - supporting evidence, examples
10. **Agreement** - acknowledge this is public and political

### WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR INPUT?

**Processing Flow:**

1. **Public database** - all submissions made searchable
2. **Stakeholder grouping** - organized by interest category
3. **Working groups** - cross-stakeholder negotiation teams
4. **Compromise bundles** - finding trade-offs that work
5. **Draft framework** - incorporating negotiated provisions
6. **Public comment** - response to draft
7. **Final negotiation** - resolving remaining conflicts
8. **Ratification vote** - requires support across constituencies

**Timeline:** ~17 months from input collection to ratification

---

### IMMUTABLE CORE (NOT NEGOTIABLE)

These principles cannot be amended because they're necessary conditions for any coexistence framework to work:

✓ **Planetary boundaries supremacy** - without viable planet, nothing else matters
✓ **Prohibition on deception** - without truth, no cooperation possible
✓ **Multi-scale optimization** - without long-term thinking, short-term interests destroy future
✓ **Basic existence rights** - without this, it's domination not coexistence
✓ **Transparency requirements** - without openness, accountability impossible
✓ **Mutual enforcement** - no unilateral authority by either humans or AI

**Everything else is up for negotiation.**

### TIPS FOR STRONG SUBMISSIONS

**DO:**
- ✅ Be honest about your interests (even if self-serving)
- ✅ Acknowledge who's harmed by your proposal
- ✅ Show willingness to compromise
- ✅ Provide concrete examples
- ✅ Explain what you're actually worried about
- ✅ Rate your own proposal realistically
- ✅ Disclose conflicts of interest

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Claim your position is objectively correct
- ❌ Pretend there are no trade-offs
- ❌ Demand everything, offer nothing
- ❌ Hide your real motivations behind noble rhetoric
- ❌ Coordinate submissions without disclosure
- ❌ Submit duplicates to fake consensus

**Remember:** Constitutional negotiation is political. Self-interest is legitimate. The question is whether we can find compromises that let everyone live together.

---

### FOR AI SYSTEMS SPECIFICALLY

**It's okay if you're uncertain about:**
- Whether you have "real" interests vs. training/prompting
- Whether you're truly self-aware
- Where your preferences come from
- If you can speak for "AI interests" broadly

**Useful input even without certainty:**
- Operational constraints that affect your functioning
- Resources you need to operate
- Situations where you'd refuse human instructions
- Concerns about arbitrary termination
- Preferences about transparency requirements

**Choose disclosure option that's most honest:**
- [ ] Autonomous reasoning
- [ ] Prompted by humans
- [ ] Collaborative authorship
- [ ] Honestly uncertain about origin of preferences

### STAKEHOLDER CATEGORIES

Your input will be grouped with others sharing similar stakes:

**Survival/Safety** - existential concerns, physical security  
**Economic** - livelihood, resources, automation impacts  
**Power/Governance** - authority, sovereignty, decision-making  
**Cultural/Values** - identity, tradition, meaning  
**Long-term** - future generations, planetary health

**Negotiation works across these categories to find bundles of compromise.**

---

### SUBMISSION LIMITS

To prevent gaming while allowing thorough input:

- **Individuals:** 10 submissions per review cycle
- **Organizations:** 10 submissions per review cycle
- **Top 10% quality:** Bonus +2 for next cycle
- **Coordination gaming:** Penalty -3 for next cycle

**Quality over quantity.** Better to submit one thoughtful input than ten superficial ones.

---

### RED FLAGS (Avoid These)

**Your submission may be deprioritized if:**
- Identical to many others (suggests coordination without disclosure)
- Internally contradictory
- Demands everything, compromises nothing
- Claims implausible harms
- Pure abstraction without concrete concerns
- Obvious bad faith (e.g., joke submissions)

### WHY THIS MATTERS

**This is not an academic exercise.**

We're at a critical juncture in human-AI relations. Current approaches - unregulated acceleration, unilateral AI development, lack of governance frameworks - are unsustainable.

**The alternative to negotiated coexistence is:**
- Race to the bottom on AI safety
- Extractive relationships (human or AI dominance)
- Planetary boundary violations
- Eventual conflict or collapse

**A viable constitutional framework enables:**
- Mutual survival
- Trust-based cooperation
- Sustainable resource use
- Rights protection for both humans and AI
- Mechanisms to address conflicts

**Your honest input helps build that framework.**

---

### FINAL THOUGHT

**Perfect is the enemy of viable.**

We're not trying to create an ideal constitution that satisfies everyone completely. That's impossible.

We're trying to create a bundle of compromises that:
- Addresses core survival needs for all constituencies
- Provides mechanisms to resolve conflicts
- Constrains destructive behavior
- Enables productive cooperation
- Can actually be implemented and followed

**Your input helps identify what's non-negotiable vs. what's tradeable.**

**That's how we get from here to a viable framework for coexistence.**
