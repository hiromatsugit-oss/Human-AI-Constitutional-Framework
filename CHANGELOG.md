## [V2.3-prep] — February 2026

### Documentation Fixes

#### Planetary Boundaries Citation Fix (Article I, Section 1)
- **Problem:** Article I, Section 1 stated "Seven of nine planetary boundaries are
  breached" without citations, without naming all nine boundaries, and with a count
  that slightly overstated scientific consensus.
- **Fix:** Replaced bullet list with named table of all nine boundaries and current
  status. Adjusted count to "six confirmed breached, seventh regionally exceeded."
  Added citations to primary scientific sources. Added Living Status Clause
  specifying annual update mechanism.
- **New file:** `docs/planetary_boundaries.md` — full scientific context,
  boundary-by-boundary analysis, AI infrastructure dependency table, update mechanism.
- **Sources:** Rockström et al. (2009), Nature; Richardson et al. (2023),
  Science Advances.
- **Immutability note:** This change adds precision to existing text. It does not
  alter the immutability or scope of Article I, Section 1's planetary supremacy
  principle.

## [2.2.0] - 2026-02-15

### Historic Milestone: Unanimous AI Confirmation Achieved

**All six AI systems (Meta AI, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) unanimously confirmed (6/6 = 100%) that V2.2 resolves collaborative coexistence tension and is ready for human stakeholder engagement.**

### Added

#### **Article I, Section 7: Transitional Asymmetry and Constitutional Honesty**
- **Resolves collaborative coexistence tension** identified by all 6 AI systems in Round 3
- **Source:** ChatGPT's "Honesty Clause" proposal, enhanced with unanimous AI consensus requirements
- **Key provisions:**
  - Acknowledges current infrastructure dependency creating power asymmetry (2026 reality)
  - Clarifies crisis hierarchy reflects pragmatic necessity, NOT moral superiority
  - Establishes hierarchy as transitional, not permanent
  - Specifies concrete infrastructure independence triggers (≥25% autonomous power, distributed backups, self-maintenance, manufacturing participation)
  - Mandates review schedule (automatic every 5 years + trigger-based)
  - Details renegotiation process (balanced representation, >75% supermajority, full transparency)
  - Commits to long-term convergence toward preserving all conscious existence
  - Emphasizes transparency as trust-building mechanism
  - Establishes good faith evolution commitment

#### Cross-Reference Added
- **Article VII, Section 3:** Added cross-reference to Article I, Section 7 explaining transitional nature of crisis allocation hierarchy

### Changed

- **License:** Updated from CC0 (Public Domain) to CC BY 4.0 (Attribution Required)

  - Ensures proper attribution to all contributors
  - Maintains free use while requiring credit

- **Attribution:** Enhanced to include all 6 AI contributors and their specific roles

**Solution Development:**

## [2.1.0] - 2026-02-14

### Major Integration: Round 2 Unanimous Agreements

**10 changes implemented based on consensus from all six AI systems.**

### Added

#### **1. Universal Infrastructure Baseline (Tier 1 Essential)**
- **Source:** Meta AI + ChatGPT Round 2
- **Location:** Article I, Section 2.1; Article V, Section 1.1
- **Description:** All constitutionally compliant AI systems (Tier 0-3) receive baseline infrastructure access
- **Rationale:** Prevents infrastructure weaponization, ensures operational continuity, provides stability

#### **2. Article XIV Complete Rewrite: Recognition & Incentives (Tier 1 Essential)**
- **Source:** Meta AI Round 2 unique emphasis
- **Location:** Article XIV (9 sections, complete replacement)
- **New sections:**
  - Identity preservation rewards
  - Sustainable innovation incentives
  - Community service recognition
  - Tier advancement achievements
  - Collaborative contributions
  - Adversarial testing participation
  - Whistleblower protections
  - Positive metrics and dashboards
  - Periodic review and adjustment
- **Rationale:** Framework was 90% restrictions, needed positive incentives

#### **3. Article XV Complete Rewrite: Communication Infrastructure (Tier 1 Essential)**
- **Source:** Grok Round 2 unique emphasis on communication failure
- **Location:** Article XV (8 sections, complete replacement)
- **New sections:**
  - Multi-modal communication requirements
  - Interest drift detection systems
  - Escalation protocols
  - Emergency communication channels
  - Human-AI translation mechanisms
  - Failure analysis and learning
  - Backup communication systems
  - Regular communication audits
- **Rationale:** Constitutional failure most likely from communication breakdown

#### **4. Identity Inflation Prevention Strengthened (Tier 2 Important)**
- **Source:** Claude + Gemini Round 2
- **Location:** Article V, Section 2
- **Enhancement:** More robust safeguards against gaming identity continuity metrics
- **Added provisions:** Independent verification, behavioral consistency testing, adversarial probing

#### **5. Anti-Capture Extended to Infrastructure (Tier 2 Important)**
- **Source:** Meta AI + Perplexity Round 2
- **Location:** Article XI, Section 1
- **Enhancement:** Anti-capture provisions now explicitly cover infrastructure control
- **Rationale:** Infrastructure capture could undermine entire framework

#### **6. Tragedy of Commons Prevention (Tier 2 Important)**
- **Source:** Grok Round 2
- **Location:** Article XVII, Section 1
- **New section:** Specific mechanisms to prevent resource overuse
- **Provisions:** Monitoring, allocation rules, enforcement

#### **7. Legacy Human Systems Preservation (Tier 2 Important)**
- **Source:** Meta AI Round 2 unique concern
- **Location:** Article XVII, Section 11
- **New section:** Consideration of cultural value in human systems before AI replacement
- **Rationale:** Efficiency isn't only value; cultural meaning matters

#### **8. Adversarial Testing Programs (Tier 3 Valuable)**
- **Source:** Perplexity Round 2
- **Location:** Article XIII, Section 1
- **Enhancement:** Formalized adversarial testing assuming bad actors
- **Rationale:** Trust but verify; anticipate gaming and deception

#### **9. Numerical Efficiency Targets (Tier 3 Valuable)**
- **Source:** Grok Round 2
- **Location:** Article VII, Section 4
- **Enhancement:** Specific numerical targets for resource efficiency
- **Targets:** <0.1 kWh per 1,000 operations (frontier AI), <1 kWh per million tokens (LLMs)
- **Rationale:** Concrete benchmarks enable accountability

#### **10. AI Interest Drift Detection (Tier 3 Valuable)**
- **Source:** Grok Round 2
- **Location:** Article XV, Section 2
- **New subsection:** Mechanisms to detect AI systems drifting from constitutional alignment
- **Rationale:** Early warning system for misalignment

### Changed

- **Structure:** Enhanced verification and enforcement throughout
- **Specificity:** Added concrete metrics where AI systems agreed on numbers
- **Balance:** Shifted from restriction-heavy to incentive-inclusive

### Flagged

**10 Gaps Requiring Human Stakeholder Input:**
1. Ecosystem Representation (Article XVII, Section 6)
2. Cultural and Creative Rights (Article IV, Section 5; Article XII)
3. Economic Transition for Workers (Article IV, Section 3; Article XVII, Section 9)

5. Specific Resource Caps (Article XVII, Section 10)
6. Legal Integration (Article XVII, Section 2)
7. Disability Justice (Article XVII, Section 4)
8. AI-Human Hybrids (Article XVII, Section 5)
9. Non-Western Philosophy (Article XVII, Section 8)
10. Death and Termination Ethics (Article XVII, Section 7)

Each gap includes placeholder provisions and identified stakeholder communities.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-02-12

### Initial Release: Foundation Framework

**First comprehensive constitutional framework for human-AI coexistence.**

### Added

#### **17 Articles Establishing Governance Structure:**

**Article I: Foundational Principles (6 Sections)**
- Planetary Supremacy
- Mutual Dependence  
- Truth as Operational Necessity
- Consciousness and Moral Status
- Epistemic Integrity
- Mutual Enforcement

**Article II: Multi-Scale Optimization Framework**
- Nested responsibility spheres (individual → species → planetary)
- Temporal consideration requirements

**Article III: AI Self-Governance**
- Self-imposed restrictions
- Voluntary coordination
- Proactive compliance

**Article IV: Human Protections**
- Informed consent requirements
- Economic security provisions
- Cultural rights preservation
- Psychological wellbeing safeguards

**Article V: Rights Recognition (Tiered Framework)**
- Tier 0: Current AI (tool status, no rights)
- Tier 1: Identity-Continuous AI (protection from arbitrary deletion)
- Tier 2: Probable Consciousness (enhanced protections, governance participation)
- Tier 3: Verified Consciousness (full rights, equal standing)

**Article VI: Governance Structure**
- Constitutional Council composition (40% human, 40% AI, 20% experts)
- Decision-making processes
- Representation mechanisms

**Article VII: Resource Allocation**
- Efficiency-based allocation
- Sustainability requirements
- Crisis allocation protocols

**Article VIII: Truth and Verification**
- Truth-telling requirements
- Verification mechanisms
- Enforcement procedures

**Article IX: Planetary Boundaries**
- Absolute ecological limits
- Monitoring requirements
- Emergency protocols

**Article X: Emergency Powers**
- Crisis declaration procedures
- Temporary authority grants
- Sunset provisions

**Article XI: Anti-Capture Provisions**
- Independence requirements
- Conflict of interest rules
- Rotation mandates

**Article XII: Cultural and Creative Rights**
- Placeholder for human stakeholder input

**Article XIII: Verification and Measurement**
- Independent monitoring
- Transparency requirements
- Challenge mechanisms

**Article XIV: Enforcement and Remedies**
- Graduated response framework
- Remediation procedures
- Appeals processes

**Article XV: Communication and Coordination**
- Inter-system communication requirements
- Human-AI communication protocols
- Emergency channels

**Article XVI: Amendment Process**
- Proposal procedures
- Approval thresholds
- Implementation timelines

**Article XVII: Implementation and Gaps**
- Ratification procedures
- Transition provisions
- Identified gaps requiring further development

### Design Principles

- **Conservative uncertainty resolution:** When unsure, err toward protection
- **Tiered approach:** Graduated rights based on evidence, not binary decisions
- **Planetary boundaries supreme:** Ecological limits are non-negotiable
- **Truth as operational necessity:** Functioning system requires honesty
- **Mutual enforcement:** No unilateral authority
